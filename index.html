02

Activity 2 · 3 minutes · BAC Chairperson

Call to Order, Welcome Remarks, and Acknowledgment of Participants

“Good morning once again.

On behalf of the Bids and Awards Committee BAC Chair, I would like to welcome to today's post-qualification evaluation for the Supply and Delivery of Oriented Polyvinyl Chloride or PVC-O Pipes for Parallel Transmission Lines, Phase 5, under Project ID No. 2026-004.

For this procurement, we have one participating bidder, Water Industries Network Corp., with an evaluated bid amount of Two Million One Hundred Eleven Thousand Thirty-Five Pesos and Sixty-Eight Centavos, or ₱2,111,035.68.

Being the sole bidder does not automatically qualify the company for the award. It remains our responsibility to verify that all requirements of the bidding documents have been satisfied.

With the necessary BAC quorum confirmed, I hereby call this meeting to order.”

03

Activity 3 · 5 minutes · BAC Chairperson

Purpose, Scope, and Applicable Post-Qualification Procedure

“The purpose of today's meeting is to verify the qualifications and responsiveness of the sole bidder based on the requirements provided in our Bid Document.

Specifically, we will focus on three major areas:

First, the verification of legal and eligibility documents.

Second, the verification of financial capacity and supporting documents.

Third, and equally important, the verification of technical compliance, particularly the specifications, certifications, and pipe test results for the PVC-O pipes (will be provided after the NOA/PO).

Our evaluation will follow the prescribed non-discretionary pass/fail criteria. We must ensure that our findings are supported by authentic documents and properly recorded.

Our goal is to determine whether the bidder has satisfactorily complied with all requirements and whether the BAC may recommend the award of the contract.

With that, let us proceed to the verification of documents.”

07

Activity 7 · 3 minutes · BAC Chairperson

Summary, Declaration and Recommendation for Award, and Adjournment

Read the following statement ONLY after the BAC has completed verification and formally adopted a favorable post-qualification resolution.

“After completion of the post-qualification evaluation for the Supply and Delivery of Oriented Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC-O) Pipes for Parallel Transmission Lines (Phase 5), Project ID No. 2026-004, the Bids and Awards Committee of Buenavista Water District acknowledges the findings and recommendations of the Technical Working Group.

Based on the verification of the bidder's legal, technical, and financial qualifications, including the applicable manufacturer's certifications and pipe test results, the BAC finds that Water Industries Network Corp., being the sole bidder, has satisfactorily complied with the requirements of the bidding documents.

Accordingly, by virtue of the BAC's duly adopted resolution, the bid of Water Industries Network Corp. is hereby declared the Single Calculated Responsive Bid (SCRB), with a total bid amount of Two Million One Hundred Eleven Thousand Thirty-Five Pesos and Sixty-Eight Centavos (₱2,111,035.68).

The BAC hereby recommends the award of the contract to Water Industries Network Corp., subject to the approval of the Head of the Procuring Entity and compliance with the remaining procurement requirements.

We extend our appreciation to the members of the Bids and Awards Committee, the Technical Working Group, the BAC Secretariat, and the End-User Representatives for their diligence and commitment to a fair, transparent, and accountable procurement process.

With that, our post-qualification proceedings are hereby adjourned.

Thank you, and good day.”
